02 What CLRT does, and what it does not

CLRT gathers coursework and class times into one view and reminds you before things are due. It draws on three sources: the calendars already on your device, a Canvas calendar feed you supply, and syllabus files you add.

Each of those can be incomplete or wrong, and CLRT can misread them:

  • A Canvas feed reflects what your school published to it. Assignments added late, changed, or never posted to the calendar will not appear.
  • Due times in a calendar feed are whatever the school encoded, which is not always the true deadline.
  • Reading dates out of a syllabus file is interpretation, not extraction. It will sometimes take the wrong date from a line, or miss one entirely.
  • Reminders depend on iOS delivering them, and on notification permission, battery state and Focus modes outside the app's control.

Treat CLRT as a second pair of eyes, never the only pair. The consequences of a missed deadline are yours, and you are responsible for confirming anything that carries weight against Canvas or your instructor directly.
