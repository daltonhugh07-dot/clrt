Coursework, classes and deadlines on one screen

The short version

CLRT gathers your classes and assignments into one place and reminds you before things are due. It reads the calendars already on your iPhone, imports a Canvas calendar feed, and pulls dates out of syllabus PDFs.

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account and no server.

01 Finding your Canvas feed link

This is the single most useful thing you can set up, and the least obvious.

  1. Open Canvas in a browser and go to Calendar.
  2. Look for Calendar Feed at the bottom of the right-hand sidebar.
  3. Copy the link it shows you. It ends in .ics.
  4. In CLRT: Settings → Canvas import, paste, and fetch.

Treat that link like a password. Anyone who has it can read your assignment calendar. CLRT stores it on your phone and your phone fetches the file directly from your school — it is never sent anywhere else.

If your school has the feed turned off, or you are on Blackboard or Moodle, add coursework by hand from the Tracker or drop in a syllabus instead. Every part of CLRT works without Canvas.

02 Common questions

Today is empty

CLRT reads the calendars already on your iPhone, so if calendar access is off, or there is genuinely nothing on your calendar, there is nothing to draw.

Check Settings → Apps → CLRT → Calendars on your iPhone. If you would rather not grant access at all, enter your class times under Settings → Class schedule in CLRT and they will show up on Today regardless.

My Canvas assignments did not come through

A feed only contains what your school published to it. Assignments added late, changed afterwards, or never posted to the calendar at all will not appear — this is the gap the syllabus import exists to cover.

A due time looks wrong

CLRT shows the time your school put in the feed. Some Canvas installations encode a time slightly earlier than the real deadline.

Check it against Canvas, and edit the item in CLRT if you want it to read differently. Anything you edit by hand is kept when you re-sync — a later import will not overwrite your correction.

The syllabus scan missed things, or got them wrong

Reading dates out of a document is interpretation rather than extraction. CLRT looks for dates near words like due, exam, submit and deadline. A syllabus laid out as a grid of weeks will confuse it, and a scanned one depends on how cleanly it was scanned.

Everything it finds is shown to you before anything is saved, so you can uncheck what is wrong and edit the rest.

Reminders are not arriving

Three things to check, in order: notifications are allowed for CLRT in iPhone Settings; reminders are switched on inside CLRT under Settings → Reminders; and a Focus mode is not filtering them out.

iOS also caps how many pending notifications any app may have at once. CLRT budgets that cap deliberately, prioritising the nearest deadlines, so something six weeks out may not have a reminder scheduled yet.

I got a new phone, or my coursework disappeared

CLRT keeps everything in a file on the device. That file is included in an iPhone backup, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings your term with it.

Deleting the app deletes its data permanently. There is no copy anywhere else, because there is no server.

Can I use CLRT on my iPad too?

Yes, but each device keeps its own copy — there is no syncing between them yet.

03 Reporting something broken

Email daltonhugh07@gmail.com.

What helps most: your iPhone model and iOS version, what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead. If it involves a syllabus or a Canvas feed, say which school — feeds differ between them, and that is usually the answer.

Please do not send your Canvas feed link. It is a credential, and it is never needed to diagnose anything.
