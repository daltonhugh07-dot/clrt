Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Effective 17 September 2026

The short version

CLRT has no server, and the developer collects nothing. Your courses, deadlines, class times, to-dos and settings are written to a file inside the app's own private storage on your iPhone and stay there.

The app makes exactly one network request in its entire lifetime, and it is not to us: when you paste a Canvas calendar feed link, your phone fetches that file directly from your school.

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code of any kind in the app.

01 Who this covers

This policy covers the CLRT iPhone and iPad app. CLRT is made by an independent developer, not a company, and has no website that collects anything — this page is a static document.

Because CLRT stores everything on your own device and operates no server, most of what a privacy policy usually describes — accounts databases, logging, data sharing, retention schedules — does not exist here. The table below is the whole picture.

02 Everything the app touches

Each row is something CLRT reads or stores, and where it goes.

WhatWhere it goesSent to developer
Courses, coursework, exams, class meeting times, to-dos, completed items, theme and notification settings A file in the app's private Application Support directory on your device. Included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone. Never
Your calendars Read only, on the device, through Apple's EventKit, and only if you grant permission. This includes any Google or school calendar you have already added in iOS Settings. Nothing is copied out of the calendar database. Never
Canvas calendar feed link Stored on your device. Your phone requests that file directly from your school's servers over HTTPS. Treat this link as a password — anyone holding it can read your assignment calendar. Never
Syllabus files you add Copied into the app's private storage and read on the device using Apple's PDFKit and Vision frameworks. Text recognition happens entirely on the iPhone. Never
Reminders Scheduled with iOS's local notification system. There is no push server, so the content of a reminder never leaves the device. Never

03 There is no account

CLRT has no sign-in, no account, and no way to create one. You are not identified to anyone, because there is no one to be identified to.

That is a decision rather than an omission. An account only earns its place when it does something — syncing between your phone and your iPad, say — and until CLRT does that, an account would be a login screen standing between you and your own coursework, collecting a name for no reason.

If syncing is ever added it will need one, and this policy will be rewritten before that ships.

04 Third parties

One, and only when you ask for it:

  • Your school, when the app fetches your Canvas feed. That request is between your phone and them, and is subject to their privacy practices.

There are no analytics services, no crash reporters, no advertising networks, and no third-party SDKs bundled in the app.

05 Keeping and deleting your data

CLRT keeps what you put into it until you remove it. There is no retention schedule because there is no server holding anything to retain.

  • Delete individual courses, coursework, to-dos or syllabus files inside the app.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored, at once and permanently.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, your CLRT data is inside that backup and governed by whatever covers the backup itself.

06 Children

CLRT is built for students managing their own coursework and is not directed at children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age, so there is nothing gathered from a child to delete — but if you believe a child has provided information through the app, please get in touch.

07 Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you, and to know whether it is sold.

CLRT holds none. Nothing about you is transmitted to the developer, so there is no file to request, no profile to correct, and nothing that could be sold or shared. Your data is already entirely in your hands, on your own device.

08 Changes to this policy

If CLRT ever gains a feature that changes any of the above — syncing, accounts that reach a server, or anything that sends data anywhere — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, with a new version number and date at the top, and the app will tell you.
